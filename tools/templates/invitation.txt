We are building two vocabularies that link survey questions and register variables across European data to shared concepts, so that researchers can find comparable measures across surveys, countries and registers. Before anyone relies on them, we want experts to check how accurate they are.

What it involves
You will see one item at a time, a survey question or a register variable, next to the concept it has been linked to. You say whether the item measures that concept, and sometimes whether a comparison with a similar item is right. A batch has 25 items and takes about 15 minutes. You can stop at any point and come back, and "unsure" is always a valid answer.
